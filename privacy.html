Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Usevanta approaches information and privacy on this website.

Last updated: September 23, 2026

1. Information we collect

The current Usevanta website is a static website and is designed to minimize data collection. We do not intentionally request account information, payment information, or other personal information simply to browse the current site.

2. Information users voluntarily provide

If you contact Usevanta by email, you may voluntarily provide information contained in your message, including your email address, name, and any other details you choose to include. We use that information to respond to your communication and handle the matter you contacted us about.

3. Website usage information

The website itself does not intentionally create a user account or require personal information to use its current search interface. Your browser, device, network, or hosting provider may process technical information as part of normal web delivery. Such processing may be controlled by the relevant provider rather than by Usevanta.

4. Cookies

The current site does not intentionally set non-essential tracking cookies. Your browser or other services may use their own mechanisms outside our control.

Essential cookies

If essential cookies or similar technologies become necessary for a future feature, they will be used only as reasonably needed to provide or secure that feature.

Analytics

No specific analytics service is represented as being installed on this version of the website. If analytics are introduced, this policy will be updated to describe the relevant data practices.

Advertising and third-party services

No advertising network, Google AdSense, Meta Pixel, or similar third-party advertising service is represented as being installed on this version. If advertising, analytics, or other third-party services are introduced, this policy will be updated to describe the relevant data practices, subject to applicable requirements.

5. Third-party vendors

Future features may rely on third-party providers such as hosting, analytics, advertising, security, or other service providers. Where such providers are introduced and their processing is relevant, this policy will be reviewed and updated.

6. How information may be used

Information voluntarily provided to Usevanta may be used to respond to inquiries, consider suggestions, maintain site security, and operate or improve the website. We do not describe any current data practice that is not actually in use.

7. Data sharing

We do not intentionally sell personal information. Information may be shared when necessary to provide a requested service, comply with applicable law, protect the website or users, or respond to a valid legal process. Future third-party integrations will be reflected in an updated policy where appropriate.

8. Data security

Reasonable measures may be used to protect information under Usevanta's control. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data retention

Email communications may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to the inquiry, maintain appropriate records, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations.

10. User choices

You can choose not to send personal information to Usevanta, except where information is necessary for a particular service you request. You can also contact us about questions concerning information you voluntarily provided.

11. External links

Future versions of the site may link to external websites. External websites have their own privacy practices, and Usevanta is not responsible for their policies or content.

12. Children's privacy

Usevanta does not intentionally seek personal information from children through the current website. If a parent or guardian believes a child has provided personal information to Usevanta, they may contact us to request that the information be addressed as appropriate.

13. Policy updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website's features, data practices, analytics, advertising, cookies, or third-party services change. The updated date at the top of this page will be changed when material revisions are made.

14. Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to usevanta00@gmail.com.
